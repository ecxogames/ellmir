## What's coming
Currently, this page is not completed because Ecxo Interactive is undergoing a massive restructuring.

It's like a complete cell rebirth cycle, after almost 4 years, we have made so much that we need to rebuild the base of the tower or it's likely to crumble.

If you have been following us for long, you would know how it was before. Poor infrastructure, slow servers, unsecure, basically everything was unfinished. Well, we have been working hard in the past year to forget about the old way and try out the industry standardized agentic development methodology to be able to release more and better updates.

We are aware that some people may be questionning the use of AI in software development, and we do too, but it's important to know that all the thinking, and hand crafted work (design, plannification, writing, storytelling, and more) is still handled by humans, we just hand over boring tasks to AI agents, that follows our very strict protocols.
